About Me

Building the Future of HRI

LC

I'm a PhD researcher at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, specializing in Human-Robot Interaction with a focus on gaze-based communication. My work bridges computer vision, cognitive science, and robotics to create more natural and intuitive ways for humans to interact with robots.

My research explores how robots can understand and respond to human gaze patterns, enabling hands-free interaction that feels as natural as making eye contact with another person. I develop novel algorithms that process visual attention in real-time, allowing robots to infer human intentions and engage in more meaningful collaborative tasks.

Through my work, I aim to make human-robot interaction seamless, accessible, and intuitive, opening new possibilities for assistive robotics, collaborative manufacturing, and social robotics.

Research Focus

Core Areas

My research spans multiple disciplines to create intelligent, gaze-aware robotic systems

👁️

Gaze Estimation

Developing robust algorithms for real-time gaze tracking in challenging HRI scenarios, including boundary condition analysis and appearance-based methods.

🤖

Human-Robot Interaction

Creating natural interaction paradigms that leverage human gaze as a primary communication channel for collaborative tasks and social engagement.

🧠

Attention & Cognition

Investigating how visual distractors affect human attention during robot interaction and developing systems that account for cognitive load.

⚡

Real-Time Systems

Building efficient processing pipelines for gaze target annotation and estimation that operate in real-time on robotic platforms.

🎯

Computer Vision

Applying state-of-the-art computer vision models to extract meaningful gaze information from video streams in unconstrained environments.

🔬

Experimental Design

Conducting rigorous user studies to validate gaze-based interaction methods and benchmark algorithm performance in real-world scenarios.
