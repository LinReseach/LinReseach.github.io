Linlin Cheng

PhD Candidate, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. I study gaze-aware human-robot interaction, focusing on low-cost gaze estimation and attention-aware social robots.

Gaze-Aware Human-Robot Interaction

I design calibration-free gaze systems that allow social robots to infer human attention and autonomously manage interaction flow in real time.

Paper Demos

Research Contributions

  • Demonstrated that appearance-based gaze models reliably capture attention patterns in live human-robot interaction.
  • Identified tablet interaction as a dominant attentional attractor in multimodal HRI.
  • Developed a real-time gaze-based method for detecting task completion without explicit user input.
  • Built reproducible pipelines for gaze annotation and analysis in robotic systems.

Selected Projects

GARI, Gaze-Aware Robot Interaction

A low-cost, calibration-free system that detects task completion through gaze shifts from a robot tablet to its face.

Multimodal Gaze Tracking in HRI

Evaluation of deep learning gaze models against eye-tracker glasses in live interactions with the Pepper robot.

Gaze Target Annotation

Automatic annotation of gaze targets using robot-centric camera geometry and appearance cues.

Gaze Following for Social Robots

Enabling robots to follow human gaze to support shared attention and engagement during interaction.

About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, working on enabling social robots to perceive and respond to human attention. My research bridges human perception, machine learning, and embodied interaction.

I am particularly interested in how subtle, often unintentional gaze behavior can be used to make interaction with robots more natural and less cognitively demanding.
